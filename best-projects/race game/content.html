Race Game

This demo was created as part of a university project, in it, my objective was to explore the DirectX11 SDK and try to make a game from the ground up.

Description

In this game, I explored the basic mechanics of DirectX 11 as well as create a mini-demo.

Mechanics explored:

  • Rendering
  • Shaders
  • Constant Buffers
  • Matrices
  • Z-Buffer
  • Cameras and Depth
  • Textures
  • Lights
  • Model Loading
  • Orientation
  • DirectX input
  • SkyBoxes
  • Particles
  • Scenes
  • Collision

On the demo, you will be able to complete a lap on the track and give you the time you took to make the lap. During the track, you will find boosters that can accelerate you in a good or bad way depending on how you use them.

To move you use the W,A,S,D keys and Shift as your own boost.
