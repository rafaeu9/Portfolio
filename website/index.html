About Me.

I’m a First-Class (Hons) Computer Games graduate turned Software Engineer at Bridgeworks Ltd, where I design and build scalable, high-performance software solutions. My work spans full-stack and systems development in C, JavaScript, and Lua, alongside troubleshooting complex WAN environments using Fibre Channel, iSCSI, and packet-level analysis. I recently containerised our flagship WAN-acceleration product, PORTrockIT, into a Docker-based microservices architecture, improving scalability, maintainability, and deployment efficiency. I also collaborated with OCF to enhance IBM’s GPFS storage performance, presenting our results at the GPFS User Group at IBM. I thrive in agile, collaborative teams and love turning intricate technical challenges into clean, user-focused solutions, especially in containerization, high-performance storage, and full-stack systems.
